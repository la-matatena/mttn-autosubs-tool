Two modes, one click

Switch modes from the top bar and step 1. Audio de origen adapts.

DaVinci Resolve 20: export your timeline audio with a script, click Leer buzón, and when you're done push subtitles back as a native subtitle track or Text+ clips.

Standalone: drop in a local video or audio file and export files or an MP4, no Resolve required.

Real Spanish, not translated

In 3. Español, dominio y reglas RAE you pick the topic, accent, and format of your subtitles.

  • Topics like Juegos de Mesa + Anglicismos (BGG) repair terms such as meeple or deckbuilding.
  • Neutral, Spain, Mexico / LatAm, and Río de la Plata accents.
  • Custom glossary for titles, publishers, and names.
  • Text rules: characters per line, ¿ and ¡ repair, and filler-word cleanup.

Frame-accurate editing

Review each cue in the Monitor previo and adjust timings directly on the waveform.

  • Drag blocks to move them, or their edges to change in and out points.
  • Zoom with +/−, ⌘ + scroll, or trackpad pinch.
  • In the editor: search and replace, split, merge, and delete cues with live CPS.

Export to any format

.SRT Estándar and Render MP4 are always visible; everything else is under Más formatos.

  • Subtitles: .SRT (also with 01:00 TC), .VTT, styled .ASS, FCPXML 1.9, and word-level .JSON.
  • Scripts: clean .TXT, timestamped .TXT, or copy to the clipboard.
  • H.264 MP4 with burned-in subtitles.

Tuned for your Mac

The Rendimiento de esta Mac panel detects your machine and picks threads, GPU, and precision for you. It runs from the 2017 Intel iMac to Apple Silicon M1–M4.

  • Automatic profile: uses the whole Mac and yields cores while DaVinci Resolve is open.
  • Safe, Maximum, and Custom profiles.
  • Speed test with 30 s of Spanish speech.
  • Portable preferences to carry your style from a MacBook to an iMac.

Get started in minutes

Requires macOS, Python 3.10+, and Homebrew. DaVinci Resolve is optional.

  1. Download

    Download MTTN_AutoSubs_Tool.zip from GitHub Releases or clone the repository.

  2. Install

    The installer sets up whisper-cpp and ffmpeg through Homebrew, creates the macOS app, copies the Resolve scripts, and downloads the Spanish model.

    ./install_imac.sh

  3. Open the studio

    Launch MTTN AutoSubs Tool from Applications or transcribe from Terminal:

    python3 -B autosubs_server.py \
  --cli video.mp4 --model turbo-q8 \
  --output-srt video.srt
